How Severance Taxes Are Calculated

Frequently Asked Questions

How is severance pay taxed by the IRS?

The IRS classifies severance pay as supplemental wages, subjecting it to a mandatory flat 22 percent federal withholding rate up to 1 million dollars.

Do I have to pay Social Security and Medicare taxes on severance pay?

Yes, severance pay is subject to standard FICA taxes including 6.2 percent Social Security up to the annual wage cap and 1.45 percent Medicare tax.

Will I get a tax refund if too much was withheld from my severance check?

Yes, because flat supplemental withholding rates may exceed your actual effective tax bracket, any excess withholding is refunded when filing your annual federal tax return.
